Inventory Administration & Control

Know Exactly What Is on Your Shelves

Helping business owners understand inventory, identify physical vs. recorded differences, recognize improvement areas, and establish consistent counting practices.

Packages may be engaged individually or combined based on your business size, SKU count, record availability, and level of support required. Pricing is provided after an initial diagnostic review.
1

Inventory Checkup (Diagnostic)

Initial assessment of inventory management practices, records, and procedures to establish a clear starting point.

What's Included:
  • Initial site visit & prior diagnostic review of inventory, records, and systems
  • Interview with the owner or person responsible for inventory
  • Review of purchasing, receiving, storage, and sales processes
  • Review of product samples and available inventory records
  • Identification of operational risks and improvement opportunities
Client Deliverables:
  • Summary of current inventory practices
  • Key weaknesses and risks identified
  • Priority recommendations & suggested action plan
2

Physical Count & Reconciliation

Comprehensive coordination and supervision of your physical inventory counting process with variance reconciliation.

What's Included:
  • Planning meeting to define scope, areas, and count cut-off date/time
  • Organization of products by department or category
  • Preparation of count sheets or digital data-capture tools
  • Physical count supervision of products in agreed scope
  • Reconciling physical counts against system balances to identify discrepancies
Client Deliverables:
  • Structured electronic data file formatted for easy system upload/integration
  • Comprehensive final inventory report presenting findings & variances
Scope Note: DLM coordinates, organizes, and supervises. Physical counts are performed by client personnel (separate counting labor can be quoted upon request). Available monthly, quarterly, annually, or as needed.
3

Inventory Valuation & Analysis

Ensuring inventory amounts and costing systems comply with your applicable financial reporting framework.

What's Included:
  • Review of inventory recognition and indirect cost allocation
  • Cost determination for items within agreed scope by turnover and risk
  • Review of controls supporting inventory valuation
  • Inventory Allowance Review: analysis of obsolete, damaged, or slow-moving items and calculation of potential valuation adjustments
Client Deliverables:
  • Inventory Valuation Assessment with identified deficiencies
  • Inventory Cost Determination calculations & allowance documentation
4

Internal Control Design & Improvement

Evaluation and enhancement of operational controls through identification of operational risks and segregation of duties.

What's Included:
  • Assessment of existing inventory policies & procedures
  • Definition of roles, responsibilities, and segregation of duties
  • Controls to prevent/detect shrinkage, errors, and unauthorized transactions
  • Enhancement of inventory reporting and management monitoring
  • Support implementing and monitoring recommended changes
Structured Cycle:
  • Available monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually
