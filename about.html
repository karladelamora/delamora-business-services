Meet Our Team

Personalized local dedication backed by world-class consulting rigor and over two decades of institutional experience.

Karla De La Mora

Founder

As the founder of DE LA MORA Business Services, Karla combines more than 20 years of audit and accounting experience with practical business support. A former Audit Manager at Deloitte Mexico and PKF San Diego, she has audited companies of all sizes across manufacturing, mining, hospitality, services, and construction.

By applying the same discipline used in professional audits, Karla helps businesses maintain accurate records, strengthen internal controls, and receive clear, reliable financial reporting, so they can focus on running and growing their business with confidence.

Bibiana King

Strategic Consulting Collaborator

Through her work with King Solutions Group, Bibiana brings over 20 years of executive consulting experience and a proven track record as a former Partner at Deloitte Mexico.

She specializes in strategic process improvement, operational efficiency, organizational design, internal controls, and technology-driven business transformation.

Through our collaboration, we combine our expertise to help businesses strengthen their operating models, improve internal controls, manage risks, and build a solid foundation for sustainable growth.
